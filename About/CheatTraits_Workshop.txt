[h1]Cheat Traits[/h1]

[b]17 overpowered traits that turn a colonist into a hero.[/b]

Vanilla traits nudge a number a few percent. These rewrite what a pawn is. A Ch Boxer punches a mechanoid apart bare-handed. A Ch Ascendant researches and hacks ten times faster and periodically invents recipes the game never intended you to have. Build a colony around one or two absurd standouts instead of a wall of generalists.

Every trait carries a unique ability or unlocks a building, so no trait is just a pile of stat offsets.

[h2]The Traits[/h2]

[b]Combat[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Ch Boxer[/b] - unarmed monster. 10x melee damage barehanded, and a Flying Punch that leaps across the map to land one overwhelming blow.
[*][b]Ch Tex[/b] - revolver ace. Near-perfect accuracy with a revolver, plus Deadeye: one guaranteed armor-piercing shot at anything he can see, at any range.
[*][b]Ch Tank[/b] - damage sponge. Heavy armor, permanent pain dampening, and Iron Wall: taunts every hostile within 45 tiles while taking 10% damage.
[*][b]Ch Zippy[/b] - speedster. Faster at moving and everything else, with a short-cooldown Blink for tactical repositioning.
[/list]

[b]Production[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Ch Artificer[/b] - master crafter. Every item and sculpture rolls Excellent, Masterwork, or Legendary. Reforge rerolls the quality of anything already made.
[*][b]Ch Engineer[/b] - master builder. Construction never fails, builds 5x faster, and every structure rolls top-tier quality. Retrofit upgrades buildings in place.
[*][b]Ch Digger[/b] - extreme miner. Triple mining speed and Tunnel, which instantly excavates a 3-wide passage straight through a mountain.
[*][b]Ch Alchemist[/b] - meals become buffs. Cooks perfect variants that grant real stat bonuses for hours, and brews Trail Tonic for long caravans.
[/list]

[b]Support[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Ch Doc[/b] - surgeries never fail, tend quality is always perfect, and Miracle Heal cures disease, closes every wound, and regrows a lost body part.
[*][b]Ch Ascendant[/b] - genius. 10x research and hacking speed, a learning-and-healing aura, and a Eureka event that grants normally-unobtainable recipes.
[*][b]Ch Diplomat[/b] - mood and opinion aura, plus Bond Pawns to permanently lock two colonists into near-perfect rapport.
[*][b]Ch Bard[/b] - battlefield support. A 12-tile aura that ramps up the longer allies stand in it, switchable between offense, defense, sustain, and reflex stances.
[*][b]Ch Green Thumb[/b] - plants nearby grow 10x faster, ignoring light, temperature, and season. Blight is removed automatically.
[*][b]Ch Beastmaster[/b] - colony animals become combat units via a 15-stat herd aura. Call of the Wild turns every wild animal on the map against your enemies.
[/list]

[b]Utility[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Ch Wizard[/b] - a real spellbook: Lightning Bolt, map-wide Teleport Other, Mass Berserk, and Super Soldier, which maxes a pawn's combat skills and conjures Legendary Cataphract gear for two hours.
[*][b]Ch Comfy[/b] - extinguishes nearby fires automatically and shrugs off any temperature.
[*][b]Ch Tesla[/b] - unlocks automated lightning defense.
[/list]

[h2]Trait-Gated Buildings[/h2]

These appear in the Architect menu only when a colonist has the matching trait, and only that pawn can build them.

[list]
[*][b]Floragen Core[/b] (Green Thumb) - triples plant growth in a 12-tile field.
[*][b]Comfort Node[/b] (Comfy) - holds a room at your chosen temperature with no power, glowing warmer or cooler to match.
[*][b]Tesla Coil[/b] (Tesla) - generates 750W and chain-zaps up to 4 hostiles per pulse. Stuns bypass EMP adaptation, so mechanoids stay locked down, and damage is tuned to down raiders rather than kill them so you can still capture prisoners.
[*][b]Alchemy Cauldron[/b] (Alchemist) - brews Trail Tonic: three days of near-zero hunger for caravans and sieges.
[*][b]Eureka Forge[/b] (Ascendant) - crafts Luciferium, Hyperweave, Advanced Components, and Glitterworld Medicine when a Eureka grants the recipe.
[/list]

[h2]Getting The Traits[/h2]

All 17 traits default to [b]commonality 0[/b], so they never appear by accident. Assign them deliberately with Character Editor, EDB Prepare Carefully, or dev mode.

Prefer chaos? [b]Options > Mod Settings > Cheat Traits[/b] has a commonality slider, with optional per-trait overrides. Turn it up and cheat traits start rolling on any generated pawn: your colonists, your allies, and the raiders coming to kill them. Off by default.
